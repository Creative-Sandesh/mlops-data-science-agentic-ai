# Nimbus Cloud Platform - Support

Starter customers get community forum support only.

Pro customers get email support, answered within 8 business hours. Support is staffed Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC.

Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support and a guaranteed 1-hour response time for critical (P1) incidents.

Support requests are sent to support@nimbuscloud.example. Live platform status is published at status.nimbuscloud.example.
