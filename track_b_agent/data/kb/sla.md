# Nimbus Cloud Platform - Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Uptime commitments: the Pro plan is covered by a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. The Enterprise plan is covered by a 99.99% monthly uptime SLA. The Starter plan has no SLA.

Service credits: if monthly uptime falls below the committed level but stays at or above 99.0%, the customer receives a credit of 10% of that month's fee. If uptime falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25% of that month's fee.

Claims must be filed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.

Scheduled maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC and is excluded from uptime calculations.
