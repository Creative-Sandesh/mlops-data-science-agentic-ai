# Nimbus Cloud Platform - Refunds and Cancellation

Money-back guarantee: first-time annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable, but they can be cancelled at any time and the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days and are returned to the original payment method.

Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. No prorated credits are issued for downgrades.

Enterprise contracts follow the terms of the signed contract; there is no standard refund policy for Enterprise.
