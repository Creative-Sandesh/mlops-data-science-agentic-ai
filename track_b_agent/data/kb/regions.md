# Nimbus Cloud Platform - Data Regions and Security

Nimbus Cloud runs in three data regions: US-East (Virginia), EU-West (Ireland) and AP-South (Mumbai).

Pro and Enterprise customers can choose their region. Starter accounts are always hosted in US-East and cannot choose.

Migrating an account between regions costs $0.02 per GB as a one-time fee; migration is free for Enterprise.

Deleted data is permanently purged after 30 days. Backups are retained for 35 days. Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
