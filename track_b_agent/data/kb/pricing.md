# Nimbus Cloud Platform - Pricing

Nimbus Cloud offers three plans.

Starter costs $12 per user per month. It includes up to 5 projects, 10 GB of storage per user and community forum support.

Pro costs $29 per user per month. It includes unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage per user, priority email support and single sign-on (SSO).

Enterprise has custom pricing with a minimum of 50 seats. It includes a 5 TB shared storage pool, 24/7 phone support, a dedicated account manager and audit logs.

Annual billing: customers who pay annually receive a 20% discount on the monthly price of the Starter and Pro plans. The discount does not apply to Enterprise.

Extra storage: storage beyond a plan's allowance is billed at $0.05 per GB per month.
